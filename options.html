Potential Solutions

Option 1: Ban Lottery Algorithms

    Pros
    • Protects all stakeholders' rights to create, experience, and promote safe gameplay
    • Allows developers to align closer to their ethical code of conduct
    Cons
    • Creators cannot incorporate randomness and must utilize other gameplay components
    • Removes types of work (gacha boxes and unique character demand) for artists

Option 2: Allow with Regulations

    Pros
    • Maintains eligibility and fairness for all players and streamers
    • Creators retain freedom to use random events and profit from in-app purchases
    Cons
    • Regulations are disproportionate to financial or mental status
    • Regulations may frustrate susceptible and possessive players

Option 3: Allow without "Pull" Purchases

    Pros
    • Allows player and streamer engagement
    • Allows creators' to incorporate randomness
    Cons
    • Restricts creators’ revenue, but permits profit through other means
    • Reduces player gain, as player contributions finance larger rewards
    • Hinders the gambling industry as a result of reduced interest and profitability

Option 4: Allow without Regulations

    Pros
    • Protects the creative freedom of all stakeholders
    • Increases companies and streamers' revenue opportunities
    Cons
    • Restricts players’ right to experience transparent, fair, and safe gameplay
    • Allows highly addictive, lucrative, and unfair algorithm designs
    • Creates gameplay inequity. Whale players acquire rare items and accelerate progress quicker
